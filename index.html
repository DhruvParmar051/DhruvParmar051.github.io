Background

I'm a graduate-level researcher and developer working across machine learning, systems engineering, and quantitative research. My work spans the full stack — from storage engine internals and compaction analysis to production ML pipelines and financial risk models.

I care about correctness before cleverness. If a system works but I don't understand why, that's not good enough. I build things to last: typed, tested, with failure modes considered from the start.

Currently focused on RAG system reliability, long-context faithfulness evaluation, and the intersection of ML and production systems.
